About Bunz

🔥Fresh & Tasty

We Are Bunz

Bunz is Kigali's go-to spot for fresh, flavour-packed food. Located at the KBC Building in Kicukiro, we've been serving the Kigali community with honest, delicious food made from scratch every day.

From our signature salad bowls topped with boiled eggs, grapes and cashews, to our loaded subs and refreshing mason jar mojitos — every item on our menu is crafted with care. Our motto? Fresh & Tasty.

We partner with local suppliers to keep ingredients fresh, and our team makes sure every order leaves the kitchen looking and tasting amazing.
